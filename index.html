Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your arrival, suite amenities, policies, and privileges.

What payment methods and digital transactions do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover. We also accept digital payments through PayPal and direct bank transfers for group bookings.

What are the standard check-in and check-out times?

Our standard check-in time is 3:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged based on availability for an additional fee.

Is breakfast included with my suite reservation?

Yes, all our room rates include a complimentary breakfast buffet served at our main restaurant from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM daily, featuring both continental and hot breakfast options.

Do you provide airport transfer and chauffeur arrangements?

Yes, we provide airport pickup and drop-off services. Please contact our concierge at least 24 hours in advance to arrange your transfer and confirm the rates.

What is the resort cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 48 hours prior to check-in are free of charge. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee equivalent to one night's stay.
